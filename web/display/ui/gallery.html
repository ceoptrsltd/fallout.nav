Compass: 3 styles × 2 orientations

Heading up: map and scale rotate, the car sits in the lower third, current heading is at the top. North up: the scale is fixed (N at the top), the heading needle/pointer moves, the car is centred. The chosen style is the display.compass_style setting. Dashed frame = 5% safe area.
Turn effect (heading up): on a 90° turn the compass turns immediately (minimal smoothing, ~0.1 s), the car icon rotates fast, and the map follows on a damped spring. The compass does not wait for the map: the current heading is always under the fixed top mark.
